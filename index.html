About Me.txt

Hi, I'm Maharaja.

I'm a first-year B.Tech student in Information Technology, which means most days I'm somewhere between "this makes sense" and "why is this not working". I like that stretch. It's where I learn the most.

I'm not trying to look like an expert yet. I'm trying to get good at the basics: writing code that runs, reading error messages without panicking, and finishing the thing I started.

This site is part of that. I wanted a place that feels like me instead of a template, so I built it from scratch, in one file, dressed up as the old desktop computers I enjoy looking at.
